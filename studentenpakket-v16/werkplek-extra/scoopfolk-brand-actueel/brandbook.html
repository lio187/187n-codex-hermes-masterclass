A small mark, with a role.

The Scoop

The original scoop remains our compact signature. Use it for avatars, favicons and small navigational details.

Give it room

Keep at least half the lowercase letter height clear around the wordmark. Minimum recommended width: 160 px digitally or 35 mm in print.

Use the symbol at 24 px or larger. Keep its tilt and proportions intact.

Never stretch, outline, add shadows, or arrange the letters differently. Keep the wordmark horizontal.

Lead with sky and teal.

Sky is the main field. Teal carries body text, navigation and the primary logo. Cream adds breathing room.

Let pink punctuate.

Use hot pink for large identity panels and highlights. Blush is a softer secondary surface. Avoid small cream text on pink.

Body text: teal on sky, cream or blush. Reversed text: cream on teal. Colors are sRGB; proof print conversions with your printer.
