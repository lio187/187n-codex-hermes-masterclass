# Offer Brief — Colorful Daily Fiber

Date: September 20, 2026  
Status: Internal offer hypothesis for product development and validation. Not a launch-approved offer.  
Source order: Approved [brand brief](../COLORFUL-DAILY-FIBER-BRAND-BRIEF.md), then the [research dossier](deepresearch.md) and [working avatar](avatar-sheet.md).

## Product

- **What it is:** A proposed unflavored daily fiber powder for adults who want a simpler experience than their current or former fiber routine.
- **Format / delivery:** One physical hero SKU. Countertop tub first; refill pouch under evaluation. Optional recurring replenishment alongside one-time purchase.
- **Core ingredients:** Evaluate a single-fiber PHGG formula first. Ingredient source, final formula, dose, grams of dietary fiber, directions and other ingredients are not approved.
- **Quantity:** Approximately 30 servings is the planning target. Net weight, scoop and labeled serving are not specified.
- **Customer:** Existing or lapsed fiber users dissatisfied with texture, sweetness, preparation, complexity or value. Demographics beyond adult US market scope are not established.
- **Brand character:** Bright, playful and reassuring. Tangerine orange, lilac, warm cream and dark plum are approved color directions; exact artwork and color values remain undecided.

## The Big Promise

**Proposed positioning promise, not approved advertising:** Make daily fiber an easy part of a routine you already enjoy.

This expresses the intended experience. The product must demonstrate that experience before the brand presents it as established performance.

- **Result, quantified where possible:** No quantified physiological result is supported for the finished product. Approximately 30 servings describes a proposed quantity, not an outcome.
- **Timeframe:** No time-to-benefit promise is established. A daily use occasion is a design intention; directions require formulation review.
- **Candidate benefit for review:** Support for regularity, subject to appropriate substantiation for the final product and exact claim.
- **Not available:** Guaranteed relief, universal tolerance, no-bloating promises, weight-loss outcomes, disease-treatment or medication-side-effect claims.

## Unique Mechanism

- **Name of the mechanism:** No proprietary mechanism or cleared name is established. Internal description: one suitable fiber in a familiar routine.
- **What it is in plain English:** Select the ingredient for the intended use, make preparation understandable, evaluate the sensory experience and let replenishment follow actual consumption.
- **Why it could work:** A product that fits someone's preferences and existing use occasion may be more practical to repeat. This is a behavioral and product-design hypothesis, not a demonstrated adherence effect.
- **Why it works better than existing solutions:** Not established. Compare the finished prototype with the buyer's current alternative and a close single-fiber incumbent before claiming superiority.
- **Why competitors cannot replicate it:** No defensible barrier has been established. Competitors already offer unflavored and PHGG-based products. Brand recognition, execution and customer service could become advantages, but are not exclusive science.
- **Evidence needed:** Formulation review, controlled sensory/mixing comparisons, observed repeat use and genuine purchasing at the proposed price.

The dossier's [competitor table](deepresearch.md#4-competitor-offer-and-positioning-comparison) explains the hurdle. The selected offer must earn a preference, not merely add a mechanism name to a commodity.

## Risk Reversal

- **Guarantee terms:** No guarantee is approved or currently available.
- **Recommendation for modeling:** Consider a first-tub satisfaction refund with a 30-day request window. This is a proposal, not a customer entitlement or a published term.
- **Conditions still to set:** Start of the window, opened-product eligibility, return requirements, shipping treatment, request process and refund budget.
- **Confidence story:** None established. If adopted, explain the actual policy plainly. Do not claim clinical confidence or say the guarantee proves the product works.
- **Immediate offer-design requirement:** One-time purchase should remain available; recurring purchase should be an informed choice.

## Bonuses

- **Bonus 1:** Proposed included quick-start guide covering the actual approved directions, tested mixing options, product information and delivery controls. No assigned retail value; write it only after specifications are settled.
- **Bonus 2:** None recommended at this stage. A separate habit tracker may be tested if interviews identify a need; it is not part of the offer today.
- **Bonus 3:** None recommended. Do not create a stack of arbitrary digital bonuses or claim value that has never been charged.
- **Service distinction:** Clear reminders, skipping and cancellation are core subscription functions, not inflated-value gifts.

**Value argument to test:** Paying a little more is worthwhile if the shopper prefers the actual product and buying experience. Do not claim to be the cheapest. Do not treat a stated willingness to pay as a paid order.

**Economics required:** Actual formula, pack, testing, fulfillment and shipping costs; payment costs; refunds; acquisition costs; cash required for the minimum order; and observed reorder behavior. Existing models are scenarios.

## Positioning Statement

Colorful Daily Fiber is a proposed everyday fiber brand for adults who have already tried fiber but want a routine that better fits their preferences. The development direction is one unflavored powder, straightforward product information, a colorful countertop tub and optional replenishment the customer can control. Its intended difference is the complete use and purchase experience. That preference, the formula and the price still need validation against established alternatives.

This paragraph is internal positioning, not evidence that the proposed advantages already exist.
