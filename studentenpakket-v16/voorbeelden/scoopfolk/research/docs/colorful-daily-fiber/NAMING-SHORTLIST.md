# Fiber brand naming recommendation

Date: September 20, 2026. User selected **Scoopfolk** after reviewing this recommendation. Trademark, domain and social-handle clearance remain unverified. The alternatives below are retained as decision history.

## Recommendation: Scoopfolk

Pronunciation: scoop-folk. Suggested wordmark treatment: lowercase scoopfolk, using original lettering appropriate to the supplied visual direction.

Scoop connects to the proposed powder format and the ordinary act of preparation. Folk gives the name an approachable, human character suited to an adult audience without narrowing it to one gender. The compound is easy to explain and has a concrete relationship to the product. It does not encode a digestive outcome, ingredient, flavor or medical promise.

Use a clear category descriptor alongside it: Scoopfolk / Daily Fiber. This is proposed naming architecture, not an approved finished-product label. A future refill could use Scoopfolk / Daily Fiber Refill if that format is developed. The name does not establish scoop size, dosing or directions.

The main drawback is a possible ice-cream association. Test category recognition with the Daily Fiber descriptor visible. Expansion into powders is natural; expansion beyond scoopable formats would be less intuitive.
